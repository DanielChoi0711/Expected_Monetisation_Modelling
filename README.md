# 🎵 Expected Monetisation Modelling

### Estimate Your Potential Revenue Across Music and Video Platforms

Expected Monetisation Modelling is a web-based analytics tool designed for music artists, content creators, and independent creators who want to estimate potential earnings from their digital presence.

By entering key performance metrics such as streams, views, uploads, subscribers, and engagement data, users can gain valuable insights into their expected monetisation potential across multiple platforms.

## 📌 Project Overview

Many creators struggle to understand how their online performance translates into revenue.

This platform aims to bridge that gap by providing a simple and accessible monetisation forecasting tool that combines both music streaming and YouTube performance metrics into a single dashboard.

The system allows users to:

* Estimate monthly and annual revenue
* Compare income sources across platforms
* Simulate growth scenarios
* Track monetisation readiness
* Understand potential revenue opportunities

---

## 🎵 Music Monetisation Analysis

The platform estimates potential earnings from:

* Spotify
* Apple Music
* YouTube Music
* TikTok

Users can input:

* Total tracks released
* Monthly releases
* Monthly streams
* TikTok usage statistics

to generate estimated music revenue projections.

---

## 📺 YouTube Revenue Analysis

The YouTube module provides:

* Subscriber analysis
* Long-form video revenue estimation
* Shorts revenue estimation
* Monetisation eligibility tracking (YPP)

Metrics include:

* Subscribers
* Video uploads
* Video length
* Monthly video views
* Shorts uploads
* Shorts views

## ⚠️ Current Limitations

At this stage, performance metrics must be entered manually by users.

The platform currently does not support:

* Spotify API integration
* YouTube Data API integration
* Real-time analytics synchronisation

Future versions may include automated platform integration and live performance tracking.
